GENETICS EXPERIMENT !

During our freshman year, we did this crazy DNA extraction experiment that completely blew our minds. We actually harvested our own DNA from cheek swabs, but the real excitement started when we realized we could use the results to compare our profiles with everyone else in the room. Suddenly, the whole lab turned into a giant guessing game as we tried to see who might actually share matching genetic traits. It was wild to think that the person sitting right next to you in class could end up being your closest genetic match.
